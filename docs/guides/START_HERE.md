## 💬 HOW TO USE THE CHATBOT

### **Step 1: Open the App**
- Go to http://localhost:3000
- Click **"⚡ Unified Report"** in the sidebar

### **Step 2: Generate a Report**
Fill the form with your farm data and click "Generate Report"

### **Step 3: Open Chatbot**
- Look for the **💬 floating button** in the bottom-right corner
- Click it to open the chat window

### **Step 4: Start Chatting!**

**Try these questions:**
```
✅ What crop should I grow?
✅ Is this yield good?
✅ Tell me about weather risk
✅ What fertilizer should I use?
✅ How is my soil health?
```

### **Step 5: Use Voice (Optional)**
- Click the **🎤 microphone button**
- Speak your question clearly
- The AI will respond with voice

### **Step 6: Change Language (Optional)**
- Use the language dropdown in chat header
- Choose: EN, HI, Telugu, Tamil, or Kannada
- Chat continues in selected language
